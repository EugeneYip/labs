# Thread B: clubs, associations, volunteer operations, local community administration

Research thread B, Batch 10 discovery round 1, started 2026-10-14. One bounded purpose: real, recurring jobs in clubs, associations, volunteer operations and local community administration (treasurers, secretaries, bar and kitchen volunteers, trustees, parish and village-hall committees, charity shops, food banks, community groups) that a static, no-account browser tool could do materially better than today. Not competitions, not event running.

Budget: at most 10 web searches, 8 page reads. Evidence labels: *verified* (official or primary page seen), *source claim* (a page says so), *inference* (judgment). URL given for every claim.

## Summary (ranked)

**Nothing in this domain clears the bar.** Batches 07 to 09 had already screened most of it (see "Prior-batch check"). Of the six leads tested, one is weak and five are rejected.

1. **Buying groups splitting cases (own lead): weak, leaning REJECT.** The job is real and has a clear output: sorting sheets and members' bills after a short delivery (an OFN practitioners' thread, 2017, *verified*). But purpose-built software already does the core. foodclub.org splits cases and works out what each person owes after the order arrives (*verified*). Foodsoft (open-source co-op software, *verified*; practitioners say it allocates automatically) and OFN cover parts of it. The newest first-hand complaint is from 2017, and the valuable half, members placing their own orders, needs shared state.
2. **Gift Aid claim schedule (seed 2): REJECT.** The claim has to be HMRC's own version-locked .ods, attached inside Charities Online, and HMRC itself names copying and pasting into it as a cause of "ASM" errors (*verified*). A generated .ods couldn't be proven without an HMRC login. What's left is checking a spreadsheet (the #005 pattern), and GASDS on its own is a once-a-year calculator.
3. **Club bar stocktake (seed 1): REJECT; Batch 08's screen stands.** No new evidence. Clubs that take it seriously pay licensed stocktakers for their independence (GCMA, *source claim*). No one was found struggling with the arithmetic.
4. **Community shares interest and withdrawals (own lead): REJECT.** Co-operatives UK's free AirRegister already handles the register, interest and withdrawals (*source claim*; its page returned 403). The statutory register needs storage that lasts and that the whole board can reach.
5. **Volunteer DBS identity checks (own lead): REJECT.** Verifiers already work inside the umbrella body's online system (KYP, GBG). The mistakes that matter come from looking at the actual documents, the document lists change, and a wrong "valid" is a safeguarding failure.
6. **Society lotteries, 50/50 draws, 100 clubs (seed 3): REJECT; Batch 09's screen stands.** The arithmetic is a few subtractions, and a 100 club is just a subscription ledger plus a draw.

## Log of searches and reads

**Read 1** (Gift Aid, HMRC's schedule guidance): https://www.gov.uk/guidance/schedule-spreadsheet-to-claim-back-tax-on-gift-aid-donations (*verified*, read 2026-10-14):
- Fields: title (max 4 characters), name (max 35; use a space, not a hyphen, in double-barrelled surnames), house name or number, postcode in capitals with a space, aggregated donations, sponsored event, donation date as DD/MM/YY (or the latest date in a series), amount with no £ and 2 decimals; over-claims entered as the tax over-claimed.
- 1,000 lines per schedule; "lines beyond that aren't attached"; more than one claim allowed, but one online claim must be submitted before starting the next.
- Aggregated lines: donations of £20 or less from different donors, a line's total at most £1,000, no donations linked to admission to visitor attractions, a description of at most 35 characters, the last donation's date, same accounting period only.
- One donor's regular gifts may go on one line with the most recent date, per accounting period.
- Sponsored events: "yes", the date received, the participant's total; any sponsor giving over £500 on their own line, not marked "yes".
- Non-residents: full address and country; no postcode for the Isle of Man or Channel Islands.
- **Format, the deciding fact:** the schedule is HMRC's own OpenDocument file, in an Excel version and a LibreOffice version ("don't convert between" them), its tab name (R68GAD_V1_00_0_EN) and .ods suffix unchanged, attached inside Charities Online. Its own troubleshooting: for "ASM" errors "check you haven't copied and pasted into the spreadsheet"; for other errors, check for spaces typed before or after donor details.
- *Inference:* (1) a Labs page can't safely hand over rows to paste, since HMRC itself names pasting as a cause of rejection; (2) the alternative, writing HMRC's .ods file directly, could only be proven by attaching it in Charities Online, which needs the charity's HMRC login (an account and credentials: outside Labs' limits and this batch). The page says nothing about GASDS.

## Own lead 3: DBS identity checks by volunteer ID verifiers (unscreened; "DBS tracking" was settled, ID checking was not) — REJECT

The idea: a club's or church's volunteer ID verifier checks a new volunteer's documents for a standard or enhanced check (Route 1: one Group 1 document plus two from Group 1, 2a or 2b; Group 2b documents' issue-date windows; one showing the current address; a five-year address history without gaps), and a page would say whether the documents in hand satisfy a route.

**Search 7** ("DBS ID checking guidelines route 1 route 2 volunteers ID verifier common mistakes application rejected umbrella body online system document groups"):
- The rules are official and widely restated: GOV.UK's ID checking guidelines for standard and enhanced checks (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/dbs-identity-checking-guidelines/id-checking-guidelines-for-standardenhanced-dbs-check-applications-from-1-july-2021, title only); Route 2 needs an external ID validation service after the documents, Route 3 is a last resort, else fingerprints (*source claim*, search summary); a "2025 changes" article (https://www.rippling.com/en-GB/blog/dbs-id-guidance-2025-changes, title only) and a council list "updated June 2026" (*source claim*): the lists move.
- Volunteer verifiers already work inside the umbrella body's online system: the LTA's KYP ID checker guide (https://lta.org.uk/4ab639/globalassets/kyp---id-checker-guide.pdf), Basketball England's verifier guide (https://www.basketballengland.co.uk/media/13954/fullverifierguide.pdf), Paddle UK's GBG verifier guidance (https://paddleuk.org.uk/shared-files/23854/GBG-ID-verifier-guidance.pdf), dioceses' guidance (https://elydiocese.org/content/pages/documents/dbs-identity-check-guidance-april-2025.docx) (all *source claim*, titles and summary); online platforms flag data issues at the verifier stage and send applications back to the applicant (*source claim*, the KYP and uCheck pages via the summary).

- Verdict **REJECT**: the route table is small, the verifier already works in a system that steps through it, the errors that matter are about looking at real documents, the lists change, and a page saying "valid" wrongly is a safeguarding failure (legal and safety ground). *inference* on the last two points.
