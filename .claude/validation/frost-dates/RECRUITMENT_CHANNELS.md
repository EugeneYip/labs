## Search

**Taken:**
- US queries belong to the Almanac and large publishers.
- UK queries include dedicated tools on Met Office data [SERP].

**No tool ranks** for Australian, NZ or German place queries [SERP]. Volume is unknown.

**To compete there, Frost Dates would need:**
- pre-built per-place pages;
- for Germany, a German interface [repo].

**Caveat:** the research tool's index is US-only, and localised Google results may differ [U].

## Channels by segment

| Segment | Channel | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Cool-climate Australian gardeners (Canberra, tablelands, Ballarat, Tasmania) | Community gardens, organic-grower clubs, local columns (Region Media), nurseries [V/U] | Specific organisations and sizes not verified. **The data currently fails here** |
| Inland and southern NZ gardeners | Yates NZ's community content, nurseries, weatherforum.nz frost threads [V/SERP] | Small population. NIWA is the authority |
| UK and Ireland allotment holders | National Allotment Society (about 130,000 members) [S]; council waiting lists about 109,000 (2025) [S]; forums | Crowded with free tools on better data. Warmth, not frost, decides tomato timing |
| German, Austrian and Swiss gardeners | Kleingarten associations [U] | The Eisheiligen covers most needs. English-only interface |
| US gardeners poorly served by ZIP tools | Extension offices, regional climate centres | Strong incumbents. Reanalysis errors peak in mountains |
