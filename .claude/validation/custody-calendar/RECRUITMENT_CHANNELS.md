# Recruitment channels: Custody Calendar

Comparative Research (2026-10-11). Internal planning only; nothing posted or sent, nobody contacted. Not recommended for use now (decision: STOP FOCUSED VALIDATION). Evidence: `notes/market-notes.md` §5–6.

## Search

- Pattern queries ("2-2-5-5 custody schedule", "50/50 custody schedule calendar") are served by co-parenting vendors' blogs (OurFamilyWizard, 2houses, Custody X Change) and law firms; overnight-calculator queries by law firms and calculator sites; Texas SPO calendars by law-firm generators [S].
- Generic queries ("custody calendar", "custody calendar maker free", "custody schedule template") returned spam on hijacked domains in the research tool's index [S], which may or may not reflect Google.
- No free, no-account, any-pattern visualizer surfaced. Volumes unknown. A good free page might rank for some pattern queries, against vendors with large content budgets [U].

## Channels by segment

| Segment | Channel | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Parents before agreement | Search; court self-help pages; legal-aid sites [S/V] | Episodic moment; emotionally charged; safety-sensitive. Don't approach parents directly |
| Mediators | Personal introductions; mediation associations; court mediator rosters [U] | Have free incumbent tools |
| Court self-help centers and legal aid | Institutional contacts; they publish handouts and links [V] | Slow; "make no recommendations" policies [V]; need state-correct counting |
| Parenting coordinators, attorneys | Professional networks | Poor fit: records and bundled tools |

## Communities

Co-parenting communities (Reddit r/coparenting, r/Divorce) weren't readable by any available tool. They're also high-conflict spaces where promotion is inappropriate [U].

## If ever reopened

3–4 mediators through a personal introduction (VALIDATION_PLAN.md); never recruit parents in conflict directly.
