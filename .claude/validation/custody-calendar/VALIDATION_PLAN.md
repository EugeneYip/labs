# Validation plan: Custody Calendar

Comparative Research (2026-10-11). The decision is **STOP FOCUSED VALIDATION** (VALIDATION_BRIEF.md): no human discovery is recommended. This records what a minimal test would look like if the owner ever reopens it. Nothing starts without explicit authorization. No contact with parents in conflict should ever be made casually: it's an emotionally charged, safety-sensitive population.

## Hypotheses (for the record)

| # | Hypothesis | Status after desk research | Confirming | Disconfirming |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Parents comparing schedules before agreement lack a good free way to see options on real dates | Plausible (official materials stop at grids) [V] | Mediators or self-help staff report parents struggling to compare | They say parents use Google Calendar, apps, or the mediator's tool happily |
| H2 | A no-login, no-client-data tool is meaningfully better for mediators than OurFamilyWizard's free builder | Contested | Mediators name account or data-handling friction with incumbents, unprompted | They're satisfied with the free builder or Custody X Change |
| H3 | Self-help centers or legal aid would link to a free, non-commercial, no-data tool | Untested | Staff say they'd link to it, given state-correct caveats | Endorsement rules or accuracy requirements prevent it |
| H4 | The job recurs enough to sustain use | Weak | Repeated use through modifications and holiday planning | One-time use, then general calendars |
| H5 | Post-agreement users don't need live sync | Falsified [V] | — | Sync is the most specific request in platform reviews |

## If reopened: the smallest human test

- **Who:** 3–4 family mediators (not parents), reached through a personal introduction; optionally one court self-help center or legal-aid contact.
- **Format:** 20-minute problem interviews; Custody Calendar shown only at the end.
- **Questions:** how they show parents schedule options today; which tools; what gets in the way (accounts, data, holiday rules, state counting); how often per week; whether a no-login, no-client-data tool would change anything.
- **Proceed to a short trial** only if at least 3 of 4 mediators say current tools get in the way in a way a no-login tool fixes, and would use it in real sessions within a month. **Stop** if they're satisfied with OurFamilyWizard's builder or Custody X Change, or if what they'd need (holiday rules, state thresholds, plan documents) crosses into legal-output territory.
- **Never collect** parents' names, schedules, or case details.

## Stages

- **B (validated)**: mediators using it in real sessions over a month.
- **C (standalone)**: sustained use beyond the owner's network, plus a reason for its own identity.
- **D (infrastructure: sync, records, accounts)**: can't happen in Labs, and would bring the obligations in RISK_AND_COMPLIANCE.md §8.

Given free incumbents and the legal and safety stakes, none of these is expected.
