# Competitive landscape: Day Clock

Comparative Research (2026-10-11). Internal. Full evidence: `notes/market-notes.md` §3–4, §7–8; `notes/device-risk-notes.md` §6. Labels: **[V]** read on the source; **[S]** snippet; **[U]** inference; **[V, rechecked]** confirmed directly during synthesis.

## Verdict

The plain day clock is priced at $0 by the market, hardware for orientation is cheap and well liked, and the feature caregivers ask for most (remote updates) is already free or cheap in apps, but impossible within Labs' rules. Day Clock's wedge (no install, no account, any browser) is real but thin, and its "old tablet" edge is false for iPads.

## Smart displays and family displays

- **Amazon Echo Show:** voice time and reminders, Drop In video; clock settings must be changed on the device [V]. Alexa Together, Amazon's caregiver service, was discontinued (Amazon notice dated May 21, 2025); Amazon now sells emergency response at $7.99 a month [V].
- **Skylight:** $39–$79 a year for family-updated displays [V].
- **GrandPad:** pricing unconfirmed (site blocked automated reading) [U].

## Wedge hypotheses, tested

| Hypothesis | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A browser page beats cheap hardware | **Mostly falsified** | About $60 clocks with thousands of high ratings need no setup [S]; caregivers living away prefer dedicated devices [V] |
| A browser page beats existing tablet apps | **Falsified on features; survives only on "no install, no account"** | A free same-name iPad app with nearly identical features; Calendar Clock free with remote admin; Clarity and Fashmel free [V] |
| Runs on tablets too old for current apps | **False for iPads; unknown for old Android and Fire** | Day Clock needs iPadOS 16.4; the free same-name app runs on iPadOS 15 [V] |
| Remote updates aren't essential | **Falsified for caregivers living away; survives for those on site** | The most repeated request [V] |
| "Try before you buy" | **Untested** | Distinctive, but nobody was seen asking for it in those words [U] |

## Monetization norms

Hardware one-off ($50–$80 mainstream, $120–$275 connected); services with monthly fees for remote management and video; apps freemium (free clock, paid remote features) [V]. The plain clock earns nothing anywhere.
