## Search

- No free web-based day clock ranks for "dementia clock", "free dementia clock online", "day clock online", or "calendar clock for seniors"; results are retailers, app listings, and keyword-spam pages on hijacked subdomains [S]. That spam suggests commercial search value [U].
- Autocomplete shows shopping intent ("for seniors", "amazon", "near me"), app intent ("for ipad", "android", "free … app"), and small "online" intent ("dementia clock online", "day clock online") [V]. Volumes unknown. A web page could plausibly rank for the "online" phrasings, but nothing measures their size.
