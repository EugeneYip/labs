### Thread B: breeders' pedigrees (finished 2026-10-15; 8 searches, 8 reads, 3 reads failed)

- **Inbreeding and mate choice from a breeder's own records: survives Stage B narrowly; the full gate waits on two checks** (`research/b-breeding-pedigrees.md`). Batch 09's inference doesn't hold: the "free online COI calculators" searches return are spam pages with no tool, and the Kennel Club's Mate Select works on its own records (*source claim*). The closest free tool is pawlineR (February 2026): it opens a spreadsheet, checks the pedigree's structure, and computes Wright's COI over all generations, but needs R installed, was built for cats, tests one pair at a time, and checks no dates (*verified* by the thread). Breed societies on Grassroots get Check Mate (a four-generation picture, no number) and the office's kinship analysis; the Kunekune society's Stud Adviser already ranks boars for a sow on all generations (*verified* by the thread); paid apps put a COI on a pairing (Everbreed, Evans, ZooEasy, Breeder Cloud Pro, Pigree; *source claim*). **Against it:** the breeders with most at stake already have tools built on their whole breed's pedigree, which a breeder's own spreadsheet can't match, so its figures will usually read lower and could reassure wrongly; outside registries cheap apps dominate; breeders' own accounts of the work are thin (one forum thread). **Checks before any gate:** direct evidence of breeders outside registries keeping sire-and-dam lists with no tool; and PMx, PawPeds' test matings, ADGA's tools, the free tiers of Breeders Assistant and BreedMate, and Pigeon Planner ruled out. Either failing makes it a REJECT.

### Thread A: first article inspection ballooning (finished 2026-10-15; 10 searches, 8 reads)

- **Ballooning a customer drawing and building the FAI's Form 3: survives Stage B narrowly; the full gate turns on whether uploads are really barred** (`research/a-shop-floor-fai.md`). The job is real and recurring: every drawing note and characteristic goes on Form 3, numbered to match the ballooned print, and an FAI is redone after design, process, location, tooling, or NC-program changes or a two-year lapse (GKN's 2022 supplier guidance, *verified* by the thread); Boeing adopts AS9102 and names no platform (*verified* by the thread); vendors claim 250–500 characteristics on a complex part and 2–8 hours to balloon by hand (*source claim*). **The field is crowded:** Werk24's Werkflow Studio is free ("Paid Studio subscriptions are not currently offered"; the first report needs no account), with AI ballooning, results with pass/fail, Form 3, PPAP, and Excel export, processed in the cloud (EU residency, US on request, no ITAR statement) (*verified* by the thread, vendor page); The Bubbler (GPL, desktop, "works fully offline", 19 commits) balloons by click, fills nominal and tolerance including ISO 2768 and 286 fits, flags results, and exports a ballooned PDF and a spreadsheet (*verified* by the thread); `balloon` (MIT, built from Go source) auto-balloons from the text layer and writes Form 3 (*verified* by the thread); Balloonist sells the browser-local version ("never uploaded", AWS GovCloud, Form 3 Rev C) at $360 a user a year (*verified* by the thread). **The wedge** is the only option found that is free, install-free, account-free, and upload-free at once: it matters where drawings may not leave the machine (ITAR, CUI, NDAs) on locked-down PCs (*inference*, to settle). **Against it:** each free tool lacks only one of those properties, $360 a year is trivial for a business, and a Labs page would be a manual, less capable relative of them. Feasibility is settled in the main session's spike (`research/m-main-checks.md`): pdf.js renders and reads text, and pdf-lib places balloons exactly, on rotated pages, in both engines; scans and outlined text have no text layer, so typed entry is the normal path.
- **Side leads (Stage A):** weld maps for fabricators (the same engine; one search for a free incumbent if FAI goes ahead); mapping CMM reports onto Form 3 numbers (weak: proprietary formats the paid platforms already import).

### Thread C: declined and long-open feature requests (finished 2026-10-15; 9 searches, 8 reads, 1 read refused)

- **A VAT margin-scheme stock book for UK second-hand dealers: survives Stage B; held for the comparison below** (`research/c-feature-requests.md`). Xero's idea "VAT - Automatically calculate VAT using the Marginal Scheme" (81 votes, April 2022) is "Not in pipeline", and on 6 November 2025 Xero said it "isn't in our roadmap" (*verified* by the thread); users override VAT by hand on each invoice, post manual journals, or invoice outside Xero (*source claim*, AccountingWEB threads). HMRC's current guide requires a stockbook entry for every item and six years of records (*verified* by the thread). No dedicated tool for resellers other than car dealers' systems and an Odoo add-on surfaced (*source claim*). **Against it:** a third UK money-rules tool after #034 and #036 (the pattern the brief warns against); Global Accounting already turns high-volume, low-value selling (items bought for £500 or less) into period totals, which leaves item-by-item matching to higher-value dealers who tend to have accountants or dealer software (*inference*); only VAT-registered dealers need it; the full-price, loss, and Global Accounting rules were read only in the withdrawn Notice 718.

The American Numismatic Society calls manual comparison "tedious and arduous", and one 8,000-coin study took nearly a decade; CoinsWeekly gives the die study as the way to expose transfer-die fakes (*verified* by the thread); collectors align and fade layers by hand in Photoshop or GIMP, and a CoinTalk thread asks for a tool (*source claim*, search summaries).
