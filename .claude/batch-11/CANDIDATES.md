### The main session's first screen (Stage A)

From the records and three searches. Settled earlier and not reopened: Harris matrices (Batch 09: Phaser, the field's own free browser tool), court bundles (Batch 07: BunTool, free and local), location audits and library stocktakes (Batch 09), knitting and quilting calculators (Batch 07), box-list catalogue checks to ISAD(G) (Batch 09, a weaker lead then; no new evidence).

- **First article inspection ballooning (AS9102, PPAP, ISO 13485): survives Stage A; Stage B in thread A.** Small machine shops and fabricators number every characteristic on a customer's drawing and tabulate requirement, tolerance, and result for the customer. A search found no permanent free tool: the dedicated tools are paid (Ideagen Quality Control, formerly InspectionXpert, a 14-day trial; 1factory; Net-Inspect) or a cloud service with a free supplier start (Werk24's Werkflow) (*source claim*, search results). Controlled or proprietary drawings make local processing a real advantage (*inference*, to check). Technical note: rendering a PDF in the browser needs pdf.js (Apache-2.0), which would be the site's first runtime package beyond React (*verified*, `package.json`).
- **Inbreeding and mate choice from a breeder's own pedigree records: survives Stage A; Stage B in thread B.** Batch 09 screened it out on inference only. A search found free tools only for programmers (CoeFR in FORTRAN, the R package FnR, a Python library) and nothing aimed at breeders' spreadsheets (*source claim*, search results).
- **Audit sampling (monetary unit sampling) for small audit practices and internal auditors: REJECT.** JASP for Audit, free and open source, covers planning, selection (monetary units, random, cell, fixed interval), evaluation, and writes the documentation, on the R package jfa (*source claim*, the package's manual and JOSS paper via search). A browser page from a public lab is also an unlikely tool for working papers that reviewers inspect (*inference*).
- **Seat sweeps and space-use counts (libraries, campuses): REJECT** (*inference*). Suma (free, open source, needs hosting), LibInsight (paid), and Google Forms with Sheets (free) cover it; several staff counting on different days favours a shared tool over one browser's storage.
- **Phase balancing for temporary event power (BS 7909): REJECT** (*inference*). A qualified electrician's job with safety liability, done in their own spreadsheets and apps; narrow.
- **Magazine flatplans with colour positions: REJECT** (*inference*). Digital colour printing has made colour-on-some-forms planning rare for small publications; InDesign and flatplan apps cover page planning.
- **Master key system bitting: not pursued** (*inference*). UK locksmiths mostly order master-keyed suites from manufacturers, US ones use manufacturer software; narrow and security-sensitive. Reopen only with evidence of locksmiths designing systems in spreadsheets.
- **Grazing plans and market-garden crop plans: REJECT** (*inference*). Extension-service spreadsheets and free seed-company calculators exist; Batch 07 rejected crop rotation.
- **Irrigation scheduling from Open-Meteo's evapotranspiration: REJECT** (*inference*). Farms are commercial users while Open-Meteo's free API is for non-commercial use; FAO's CROPWAT is free.
- **Plant passport labels, ADR "1,000 points" loads, room utilisation from booking exports, Zoom and Teams attendance merging, Gauge R&R and SPC, volunteer time as match funding: REJECT** (*inference*).
