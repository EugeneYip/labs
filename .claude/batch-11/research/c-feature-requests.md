# Thread C: jobs that software vendors' own users describe finishing by hand

**Purpose (one, bounded):** a discovery method, not a domain. Search public idea portals, feature-request boards, and community forums of software used by small organisations and specialists for requests that were declined, marked "not planned", or left open for years with many votes, where users say they export data and finish the job in a spreadsheet, on paper, or with a script. Directions favoured: maintenance and facilities, inventory and stock, collections management, library systems, farm and horticulture, field data collection, maker and fabrication, print and publishing, volunteer and nonprofit tools, trades job software, accounting features outside payroll and bank categorisation.

**Budget:** at most 9 web searches and 8 page reads (WebFetch). Counted below as S1..S9 and R1..R8. Read-only: no sign-ups, log-ins, votes, posts, forms, or deliberate downloads.

**Labels:** *verified* (read on an official or primary page; a vendor's own page is primary for what the vendor says about its product), *source claim* (a page or a search snippet says so), *inference* (this thread's judgment). Short quotes only (under 15 words). Findings are advisory; the main session decides and re-checks deciding claims.

**Settled families (not researched):** payroll and holiday pay; bank-statement categorisation and treasurers' year-end accounts; Gift Aid; volunteer rotas and shift scheduling; event running orders, tournaments and competition scoring; captions and scripts; file privacy, metadata and hidden-data inspection; school allocation and class mixing; exam timing; court bundles; Harris matrices; GEDCOM validation; location audits and library stocktakes; cut lists, booklet imposition, label sheets; smart-meter tariff comparison; food labels; environmental logger data.
