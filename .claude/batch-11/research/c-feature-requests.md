### S1 (search 1 of 9): Xero product ideas, VAT margin scheme
- Query: `Xero product ideas "margin scheme" second-hand goods VAT stock book`.
- A Xero Product Ideas suggestion exists: "VAT - Automatically calculate VAT using the Marginal Scheme", https://productideas.xero.com/forums/939198-for-small-businesses/suggestions/45048502-vat-automatically-calculate-vat-using-the-margin (*source claim*, search result; votes, date and status not in the snippet; read next as R1).
- Commenters (per the search summary, *source claim*): manual calculation "a bit tedious when processing large volumes"; an accountant with second-hand-market clients; "save so much manual time" and help with HMRC record keeping.
- Workarounds described on AccountingWEB "Any Answers" threads, 2012 to 2020 (*source claim*): keep a stock book outside Xero and override the VAT on each sales invoice by hand; or two-line invoices (zero-rated cost line plus a VAT-inclusive margin line), which shows the customer the margin, so some issue a separate customer invoice; or Products and Services as stock with the margin VAT added at sale. "The stock book is still necessary" for HMRC (*source claim*). URLs: https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/63342 , https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/using-xero-for-second-hand-car-business , https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/recording-second-hand-margin-scheme-in-xero
- *Inference:* this is the Xero holiday-pay pattern again (software can't do a statutory per-item calculation; the record lives in a spreadsheet). Worth one read of the idea page for votes and status, then one official read of the record rules.

### R1 (read 1 of 8): the Xero idea page
- URL: https://productideas.xero.com/forums/939198-for-small-businesses/suggestions/45048502-vat-automatically-calculate-vat-using-the-margin
- *Verified* (vendor's own portal): title "VAT - Automatically calculate VAT using the Marginal Scheme"; posted 5 April 2022 by the Xero Team admin account; **81 votes**; status **"Not in pipeline"**. Xero's Community Manager, 6 November 2025: the idea "isn't in our roadmap", pointing to manually adjusting VAT on invoices and credit notes. An earlier Xero reply (21 December 2023) gave the workaround: record the purchase as VAT exempt, sell on a tax-exclusive invoice, override the VAT by hand, hide VAT on a custom invoice template.
- *Verified* (users' comments, first page of 20, 2023 to 2025): margin-scheme invoices prepared outside Xero and the margin data uploaded (May 2024); manual journals for a couple of clients (June 2023); new VAT codes plus a manual adjustment of the return (May 2025); manual calculation "tedious" at large volumes (2023, 2025); clients "in second-hand markets", "buy and sell high-end cars"; one asks for TOMS (tour operators) too; the manual-override route was in Xero's old invoicing, retired 1 September 2024, and is missing in "new invoicing" (Feb 2025). No third-party tool named. A second comment page was not read.
- *Inference:* the demand is real but modest (81 votes over three and a half years, mostly accountants); the decline is explicit and recent. The job that stays manual is the per-item stock book and the margin VAT per quarter, not the invoice wording.

### S2 (search 2 of 9): margin scheme stock book, incumbents and templates
- Query: `VAT margin scheme stock book spreadsheet template OR software second-hand dealers eBay Vinted resellers "stock book"`.
- **Odoo app** "gb vat margin scheme second hgoods" (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/gb_vat_margin_scheme_second_hgoods): per-item stock book (stock reference, purchase date and price, seller name and VAT number, invoice references, the sale leg), low-value bulk stock aggregated by period with loss carry-forward (global accounting), and a wizard for the period's VAT on margins (*source claim*, search summary; price not seen). An ERP add-on, so only for businesses already on Odoo (*inference*).
- **Zmartly** (zmartly.co.uk), UK content for Vinted, eBay and Depop sellers: "How to run a VAT margin scheme stock book (worked example)" (https://zmartly.co.uk/news-insights/vat-margin-scheme-stock-book) lists twelve fields and says that without the stock book HMRC can charge VAT on the full selling price (*source claim*). Whether Zmartly is itself a stock-book product is unknown; read next (R2), because it may be the strongest incumbent.
- **GoFile** (https://gofile.co.uk/knowledgebase/vat/vat-margin-scheme/): a commercial VAT-filing product's knowledge page; records kept six years (*source claim*). **UK Calculator** (https://ukcalculator.com/vat-margin-scheme-calculator.html): a one-item margin calculator, formula only (*source claim*).
- AccountingWEB threads (2010 to 2016): QuickBooks route is stock control to work out each sale's margin, a quarterly report, then a journal for output VAT (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/how-do-you-record-margin-scheme-vat-on-quickbooks); a Sage thread notes the VAT can't be known until the sale; an accountant says margin-scheme clients "keep all their VAT records on spreadsheets"; another "devised" a stock spreadsheet alongside the accounts package (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/second-hand-margin-scheme-software) (*source claim*, all via search summary).
- An Amazon Seller Central Europe forum thread on the Margin/Global Accounting Scheme exists (https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/0d4fbd556468614aa2c835be3dc647b1), not read (*source claim*). Vehicles have their own notice, 718/1 (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-notice-7181-the-vat-margin-scheme-on-second-hand-cars-and-other-vehicles).

### R2 (read 2 of 8): Zmartly's stock book worked example
- URL: https://zmartly.co.uk/news-insights/vat-margin-scheme-stock-book
- *Verified* (what the page says): Zmartly is a London accountancy firm (monthly plans at £129, £250, £499), **not a software product**; it names no stock-book tool and says a stock book can be kept "on paper or digitally (a spreadsheet is fine)".
- *Source claim* (the firm's statement of HMRC's rules, to be checked against Notice 718): twelve fields (stock number, purchase date, purchase invoice number, purchase price, seller's name, description, sale date, sales invoice number, buyer's name, selling price, margin, VAT due); VAT is one-sixth of the margin at 20%; a sale below cost has a nil margin and the loss can't reduce VAT on other items, except under Global Accounting (items bought for under £500 each, VAT on total eligible sales less total eligible purchases per period); if HMRC can't verify a margin, VAT is due on the full selling price; records kept six years, and for unsold stock older than that until it sells.
- Worked example (*verified* as content): a Vinted reseller's quarter, four items (one sold at a loss), margin VAT £36.17 against £70.83 at standard VAT on £425 of sales.
- *Inference:* the record is item-level, kept for years, and built from two sources the reseller already has: purchase records (often private sellers, car-boot buys, house clearances, with no invoice) and marketplace sales exports. That is a match-and-reconcile job, not a one-number calculator.

- **Link My Books** has Vinted-to-Xero and Vinted-to-QuickBooks integrations producing payout summary invoices with the VAT (https://linkmybooks.com/uk/integration/vinted-xero) (*source claim*, vendor page via search; whether it computes margin VAT per item was not shown). Its August 2026 comparison names Link My Books, A2X, Synder and Taxomate for eBay sellers (https://linkmybooks.com/blog/best-accounting-software-for-ebay-sellers-in-the-uk) (*source claim*).
- AccountingWEB "Software for Margin VAT scheme" (2024): a practitioner had not found software that handles the margin scheme without manual adjustments (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/software-for-margin-vat-scheme) (*source claim*, search summary).
- **Car dealers are served:** MotorDesk (dealer management) says margin VAT is calculated automatically and syncs to Sage, QuickBooks and Xero; a listing shows a starter plan at 79 a month, "HMRC VAT Margin Scheme Compliant" (https://zoftwarehub.com/products/motordesk/plans) (*source claim*, third-party listing); an AccountingWEB user's dealer sent invoices to Xero via MotorDesk "with the marginal VAT done" (*source claim*). DealerDesk is another dealer system (getapp.com listing, *source claim*).
- *Inference:* vehicles have paid, dedicated systems, so they are not the audience; small resellers of clothing, vintage, antiques, books, phones and collectables appear to have no dedicated stock-book tool beyond an Odoo add-on, and a spreadsheet plus an accountant's adjustment is the visible norm. One search's absence is not proof; Link My Books' margin handling is unconfirmed.
