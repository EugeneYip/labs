### 1. Who may play down this week: England Hockey's player selection rules (section 8) for a club with several teams
- **Audience:** a hockey club's selection secretary or club captain, with the team captains who build the GMS team sheets, at a club with several adult teams in an England Hockey area league.
- **The job:** just before: the season's league team sheets are on GMS; this week's fixtures are known (which teams play, whether a higher team has no league fixture, rearranged second fixtures, which teams are in their final four league matches). Needed after: a team sheet for each team that keeps to the movement-down caps, the 50% test where it applies, the two-games-a-match-day limit and the doubling-up limits; SXP (exemption pass) requests in by 8pm Thursday (East) for anyone who falls short; then the GMS sheets before the start.
- **Frequency / importance:** every league week, roughly September to April. The 50% test applies in a higher team's bye weeks, double-fixture weeks and each team's final four matches (the run-in, when promotion and relegation are settled). A player picked without a granted exemption "will be treated as an ineligible player" (NW, *source claim*, search snippet); playing without an SXP confirmation is "at the Teams risk", and the ALMC "can determine at any time" whether a club has breached the Aims of Team Selection (*verified*, East). The penalty itself sits in an appendix not read.
- **Evidence of friction:** only a vendor's account: "frantic spreadsheet searches", "manual counting errors", forfeits, "points deductions and fines" (Teamo article, 10 Dec 2025, *source claim*). A paid vendor built and markets a feature for this (*source claim*). The rules are restructured often: South East 2025-26 renamed "Fair Selection" to "Player Selection", added a two-game-per-match-day limit and raised the doubling-up squad from 12 to 13 (*verified*); the 50% rule seems newer than 2023-24 (*inference*, F3 vs F10). So a club's spreadsheet goes stale (*inference*). No independent forum post found (US-only search; shared search limit reached).
- **Alternatives found:** Teamo Pro "Team Tied Calculator" (paid, price not seen; appearances for that team and lower divided by total league appearances, badges green 60%+, yellow 50-59%, red under 50%; uses Teamo's own attendance, not GMS; no mention of which weeks trigger the test, the final four, or the movement caps; web.teamo.chat/blog/teamtiedCalculator, *source claim*). England Hockey GMS (free and compulsory; leagues judge SXPs "on the basis of evidence supplied and GMS data", *verified*; nothing found saying it warns clubs, *source claim*). A spreadsheet.
- **Strongest incumbent:** Teamo Pro for clubs already paying for Teamo; the selection secretary's spreadsheet everywhere else.
- **Possible Labs wedge:** one page for the person who keeps the record: the season's league appearances (player by team by match week, pasted or ticked from each GMS sheet), this week's fixtures marked, and per player the Regular Team, the percentage at each level, which teams face the 50% test this week and why (higher team not playing, second fixture, final four), the movement-down cap by grade (1 team at Grades 1-2, 2 at 3-5, *verified* NW 2023-24), the two-game limit, and a list of players who need an SXP before Thursday. Saved in the browser, with a file to hand on; the area and season of the rules it follows named on the page.
- **Labs-fit problems:** GMS needs a sign-in and its export format is unknown, so appearances are typed or pasted each week (entering the same data twice). Rules are set per area and change most seasons; the full regulations and Appendices 4 and 5 (Regular Team definition, what counts, substitutes, prior-season data, the penalty) were not read. A club where each captain selects separately needs shared state; the page works only when one person holds the record.
- **Strongest reason not to build:** the sum is one ratio per player per team; the real work is the appearance record, which GMS already holds and Teamo already turns into badges. England Hockey could add a flag to GMS at any time.
- **Thread verdict:** lead, the best-defined rule found, conditional on reading one area's full regulations and finding a sign of friction that doesn't come from a vendor.

### 2. Cricket leagues' playing-down rules (the same job, heavier penalties, a different rule in each league)
- **Audience:** the match secretary or selection committee of a club with three or more Saturday XIs.
- **The job:** just before: the season's scorecards on Play-Cricket and this weekend's availability. Needed after: XIs that don't break the league's thresholds, before team sheets go in.
- **Frequency / importance:** weekly, May to September. Teamo lists ECB Play-Cricket as a partner, but its tied-player calculator is for hockey only (*source claim*). Spreadsheets.
- **Strongest incumbent:** Play-Cricket's own records, which a secretary reads by hand (*inference*).
- **Possible Labs wedge:** the same engine as lead 1 with a small set of rule types (N games for team X means not below team Y; X% of a higher team's matches bars a lower team's last N; played for X last week means not for Y this week).
- **Labs-fit problems:** every league writes its own thresholds; the Play-Cricket API needs a key, so appearances are copied by hand from public scorecards; the registration and residency rules behind the biggest penalties (if they are) aren't a counting problem.
- **Strongest reason not to build:** a rule set for every league is a large scope, and the worst documented penalties may come from registration, which this page wouldn't catch.
