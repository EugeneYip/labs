## Gate: L1 per-donor giving statements

- E1 Audience: the treasurer or financial secretary of a small US church or nonprofit with no donor system (QuickBooks Online or a spreadsheet).
- E2 Job: before, a year of gifts in a ledger, then in January one statement per donor (each gift, the total, the organisation's details and acknowledgement wording) via a pivot and a Word merge workaround, or by hand. After, open the gift list and print every statement.
- E3 Friction: verified (the QuickBooks thread, 2017 to 2024); Etsy trackers sold on "year-end receipts" (source claim).
- E4 Alternatives: CharityCharge's free local statements page (verified); Zeffy's bulk receipts (source claim); church suites (ChurchTrac's paid giving tier, Planning Center at small scale, ChurchApps B1, FlockBase); QBO add-ons (BRC Donor Statements, DonorStatements.com) (source claim).
- E5 Strongest incumbent: CharityCharge's Year-End Donor Statements: free, no account, parsed in the browser, one statement per donor, print all.
- E6 Wedge: none left. Households and IRS wording are all that's missing, and CharityCharge leaves the wording out on purpose ("does not determine tax deductibility"), which Labs should too.
- E7 Fit: good (static, CSV in, pages printed), but the gap is already filled.
- E8 Against: the same free, local tool already exists; US only; January only; tax wording.

## Gate: L2 subs payments matched to members

- E1 Audience: membership secretaries and treasurers of UK clubs and societies whose members pay by bank transfer or standing order: history and family history societies, sports clubs, WIs, u3as not using Beacon's finance tools, choirs, allotment societies, residents' associations (role descriptions and treasurer guidance, source claim).
- E2 Job: before, export or read the bank statement and work out who each credit is from, using the payer name as the bank shows it and a short reference (18 characters at most UK banks, inference). Then tick the member list, chase non-payers and query unknown credits, monthly or at renewal time. After, open the bank CSV and member list on the device. Matches are proposed from the fee amounts, one payment split across a family, membership numbers or surnames in references, and payer names learned in earlier months. The secretary confirms or corrects, and gets paid, unpaid (for reminders) and unmatched lists, plus an updated member CSV with a payer-name column.
- E3 Friction: mostly ways of coping, not complaints. References prescribed by many societies, emails asked for after paying, monthly hand entry in role descriptions; "sometimes difficult… references are often unclear" (an AGM 2026 report, snippet only); spreadsheet forum questions (Mumsnet, verified, but a business's clients; MrExcel, LibreOffice, AccountingWEB, source claim). No account of the hours it takes was found.
- E4 Alternatives: membermojo, where an admin marks bank transfers paid by hand (verified); Hello Club records them by hand (source claim); Beacon's reconciliation tools for u3as (source claim); Zeffy's free UK online dues, which avoid the job (verified); ledger matching in accounting packages; Excel's Fuzzy Lookup and Power Query; generic compare-two-lists tools (source claim).
- E5 Strongest incumbent: the spreadsheet plus a set reference (free, already in place). For groups willing to change, Zeffy's free online dues, or Beacon for u3as.
- E6 Wedge: bank statements and member lists never leave the device; any bank's CSV through column mapping; matching that knows amounts, family payments, references and remembered payer names, which lookups and list comparers don't; payer names kept in the member file, not only in the browser.
- E7 Fit: static, local CSV, one person on one device, offline. Desktop-centred, since bank apps rarely export CSV (inference).
- E8 Against: thin evidence of pain (coping rules seem to work, and no complaint about the hours was found). It sits in the files-and-lists domain Batch 08 deprioritizes, next to the rejected "compare two lists". Free options remove the job (Zeffy; Beacon; card payments in membermojo). A 100-member society takes minutes. Matching on real UK bank exports is untested (payer name formats vary by bank, inference).

## Verdicts

- L1 per-donor giving statements: REJECT. CharityCharge's free page already does it in the browser with no account (verified).
- L2 subs payments matched to members: HOLD. A real, routine job with no free member-matching tool found, but the pain evidence is coping rules, not complaints; free online dues (Zeffy) and Beacon remove it for many groups; and it's in a deprioritized domain. Reopen with a treasurer's own account of the time it takes, and a matching test on a few real, anonymised UK bank exports.
- L3 club bar stocktake: REJECT (scan level). L4 food bank stock: REJECT (scan level).
- Overall: no lead in voluntary-organisation admin clears the BUILD bar.
