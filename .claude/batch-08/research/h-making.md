## Raw facts so far (with sources)

### Camera-club competitions
- PhotoEntry (UK): subscription web app; says it handles entry collection problems (wrong number of entries, wrong image size or colour space, wrong file names, receipt confirmation), loading images into projection software, recording scores, publishing results, series results. Free one-month trial; price page not read; a price rise applies to invoices from 1 April 2026. Names no rivals. (verified, https://photoentry.uk/, read 2026-10-13)
- TalkPhotography thread 25-28 May 2020, six replies: a club with keypad scoring wanted a phone-based multi-judge system; the replies name Photo Comp and Photo EX (both "work well", neither suited phone judging), DiCentra (rival, little known), and a club member's home-built voting page. No spreadsheet complaint, no error complaint. (verified, https://www.talkphotography.co.uk/threads/photography-club-scoring-system.711486/)
- Photo Competition Manager: free WordPress plugin for online submission and voting, results dashboards (source claim, https://is.wordpress.org/plugins/photo-competition-manager/). Pixoroo: judging interface for clubs (source claim, https://pixoroo.com/localcompspublic/pixorooforclubjudges). Zealous: open-call competitions with entry fees (source claim, https://zealous.co/about/). A Drupal sandbox module handles seasons and cumulative competitions (source claim, https://www.drupal.org/sandbox/martin/1088764).
- A real rule set: Croydon Camera Club's PDI competition takes the best two of three scores each round and the best four of five rounds (source claim from search summary of https://www.croydoncameraclub.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Competition-Tables-_-PDI-_-2023-24.pdf, not read).
- An Excel Forum thread "Score sheet for photo competitions" exists (title and URL only, not read): https://excelforum.com/excel-general/963046-score-sheet-for-photo-competitions.html
- Club image rules found in search: max 1920x1080 px and sRGB; JPEG, sRGB, max 2 MB, files outside the limits rejected by the system; file name = title (source claim, search 11). Clubs publish how-to-resize pages, for example https://csmackay.myphotoclub.com.au/wp-content/uploads/sites/322/2023/11/Resize-Images-for-Digital-Competitions.pdf and https://www.sites.google.com/site/bribiephotographyclub/club-competition/preparing-your-images (not read).
- Free batch tools already do resize and rename: FastStone Photo Resizer (free for personal and educational use), Image Tuner (free), Batch Photo Resizer for Mac ($1.99); the search could not confirm any converts to sRGB (source claim, search 11).

### Choir risers and seating
- ChoralNet post, 2 Feb 2012: the author reviewed free seating-chart software for choirs; the browser-based chart he found was clunky (no saving, 15-seat row limit, 10-character names); entering names is tedious and rearranging in Word is hard; commenters use Excel; one recommends Ensemble Manager's seating module (free to ACDA members then); commenters in 2013 and 2015 still asked where to find it. (verified, https://choralnet.org/2012/02/free-seating-chart-software/)
- Chorus Connection "Riser Charts" (2016 blog): drag singers onto risers, colour-coded by voice part, shorter singers front; part of a choir-management platform (source claim, https://blog.chorusconnection.com/blog/2016/5/13/introducing-riser-charts). FirstChair: iPad app, $39, band/orchestra/choir seating (source claim, https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/firstchair/id6670373201). Google Slides riser packs and Canva/Word/Excel templates. No tool found that suggests a formation from voice parts and heights (source claim, searches 2 and 6).
- Placement guidance exists: Sweet Adelines riser placement handout (https://sweetadelines.com/sites/default/files/director/handouts/Riser%20Placement%20-%20LeVezu%202007.pdf), Harold Rosenbaum on arranging singers (https://www.van.org/articles/HaroldRosenbaumArrangingSingers20071222.htm) (source claim).

### Craft-fair organizing
- Layout/map software exists: OnePlan (to-scale festival maps), Marketspread (market management platform with a layout designer), Proxi (shareable vendor map) (source claim, https://www.oneplan.io/articles/?p=430, https://marketspread.com/learning/articles/layout-designer-howto/, https://proxi.co/landing/craft-fair-vendor-map). Application forms: Paperform and WPForms templates. A paid Payhip workbook for vendors tracks application, plan, break-even, actual result (source claim, https://payhip.com/b/X1Jmj). No free booth-assignment spreadsheet found.

### Kiln and glass
- Excel Glass Firing Calculator (free, needs Excel): plans ramps and holds and totals the time (source claim, https://fusedglass.org/?p=5653). A generic web "firing schedule calculator" under a finance-calculator site (source claim, https://www.ultimatefinancecalculator.com/calculators/firing-schedule-calculator). Digitalfire publishes schedule tables (https://digitalfire.com/schedule/127). Bullseye's guide says anneal for at least 2x the thickest section (source claim, https://www.bullseyeglass.com/wp-content/uploads/writing-firing-schedules-for-fusing-and-slumping.pdf). Lisa Vogt's tables (Stanford): https://web.stanford.edu/~dubois/stained_glass/projects/Fusing/FiringSchedules.html. No online generator that draws a schedule graph from thickness found (search 13).

### Others
- Bookbinding: guides disagree on board and turn-in conventions (2 mm vs 3 mm width allowance, and so on); generic calculators exist (https://usecalcpro.com/crafts/bookbinding-calculator); a bookbinding API exists (source claim, search 8).
- Handbells: tools list "bells used" from a score (MuseScore plug-in, Finale plug-in) but none assigns bells to ringers; assignment is by guides (Malmark's Eight-Ten system book, AGEHR guide); Handbell World says the director should assign (source claim, search 9).
- Printmaking editions: Artlogic, Artwork Archive, ArtCloud and Creativehub track edition numbers, proofs and sold status; all are accounts-based (source claim, search 10, e.g. https://www.artworkarchive.com/blog/announcing-new-edition-tracking-on-artwork-archive).
- Sewing size choice: only blog guides (blend between sizes, ease = finished minus body); no calculator surfaced (source claim, search 12, e.g. https://mellysews.com/?p=22182).
- Art-society exhibitions: a gallery supplies a "Schedule A" spreadsheet that generates the sales sheet and labels; a Canadian show supplies an Excel artworks list (source claim, search 4).
