## Scan log (leads seen, in order), after 12 searches / 0 fetches

1. Bouldering / climbing club comp scoring (tops, zones, attempts; IFSC points format; ABS-style points). Apps exist: Sendkeeper, Boulder Scoring, SteepScores (all iPhone listings; source claim), leaderboard sites KeepTheScore and Leaderboarded take an uploaded spreadsheet. No free ready-made scoring template found. Forum guide (Rocksport) describes ABS-style: flash 100%, second try 90%, later 80%, best 5 or 7 problems summed (source claim): https://rrcarchives.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=72895 . Formats differ comp to comp (IFSC tops/zones/attempts, IFSC 2025 points 25/10/-0.1 per fall, ABS points, Brooklyn league 100%/50%): https://wl.brooklynboulders.com/pages/bouldering-league , https://www.inside-climbing.com/2026/01/19/2025-reivew-part-2-boulder-points-system/ .
