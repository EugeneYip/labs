Two fetched PDFs (UGA, Gravity Vault) were read locally with pypdf. Read-only. ## E1 Audience
- University rec walls, yearly one-day comps. UGA "Boulder Bash": one day each fall, climbers from across the US southeast, 3 divisions (beginner, intermediate, advanced) each split male/female, 20 to 25 problems per division (verified; handbook reviewed July 2024, versions also exist for 2021 and 2023): https://recsports.uga.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/17/2024/08/OR_MGR-CLIMBING-WALL-BOULDER-BASH-07.2024.pdf . Others: GVSU "BYOB", 9 Nov 2025, categories by grade V0 to V6+, prizes for the top three in each (source claim): https://www.gvsu.edu/rec/outdoors/bring-your-own-beta-byob-bouldering-competition-105.htm ; LSU intramural rules 2025 and U Toledo, both on printed cards initialled by witnesses (source claim): https://apa.lsu.edu/urec/programs/intramural-sports/im-rules/bouldering-rules-2025.pdf , https://www.utoledo.edu/studentaffairs/rec/challenge/pdfs/BoulderingRules.pdf ; the first German university championship (May 2025) was run by 20 sport-science students (source claim).
- Gym league nights, 1 to 3 seasons a year. Gravity Vault Long Island, spring 2025: an adult league over 6 weeks, teams of 2 to 5 (verified, PDF): https://gravityvault.com/_media/MLVL/Docs/Spring_2025_Bouldering_League.pdf ; Brooklyn Boulders weekly league (source claim): https://wl.brooklynboulders.com/pages/bouldering-league ; Northern Rocks Auckland, 9-week "Winter Warriors" league run in TopLogger (source claim): https://www.climbing.nz/events/winter-warriors-boulder-league-2026-northern-rocks-auckland/
- National and youth series. Irish Bouldering League 2026/27: 5 rounds at 5 walls, each about two weeks, €3 or €5 per round (verified): https://www.mountaineering.ie/content/IrishBoulderingLeague/176 . The BMC youth series gives each competitor a scorecard at registration, with a judge on every bloc and up to 5 attempts (source claim): https://thebmc.co.uk/Handlers/DownloadHandler.ashx?id=2325 . USA Climbing youth qualifier events are redpoint format, at least 3 hours long, and athletes may enter as many as they like (source claim): https://usaclimbing.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/A-Guide-to-2022-Lead_Top-Rope-QEs.pdf
- (inference) The audience is real and recurring: most walls run at least one comp or league a year, with volunteer or junior staff organisers.

## E2 The exact job and the formats
- THE JOB, concretely (verified, UGA): check-in hands out a paper score card; a courtyard monitor initials attempts; a "Scorer" tallies "in Excel when climber submits a finalized score card"; the last session ends at 8:00 PM and awards are at 8:30 PM. So: one desk, one laptop, a stack of cards, and ranked lists for six categories in about 30 minutes. Gravity Vault's league final has the same shape: climbing 4 to 8 PM, awards 8:30 PM (verified).
- UGA points: each problem has a value, a flash adds 10% (350 becomes 385), failed attempts cost nothing; no tie-break stated (verified).
- Irish league: a top on the 1st try scores 10, on the 2nd 7, on the 3rd 4, and later 2, plus 1 for the bonus hold; 30 problems per category; categories Masters 45+, Senior 18+, Youth 14 to 18, by gender; results go up after each round, with a request to "check the results for any errors" before the next one (verified).
- IFSC 2025 points: 25 for a top, 10 for the zone, 0.1 off for every unsuccessful attempt; a flash is 25, a top on the 2nd go 24.9 (verified, World Climbing news, 5 Mar 2025; the page gives no tie-break): https://www.worldclimbing.com/news/new-rule-changes-take-effect-for-ifsc-world-cup-series-2025 . Zone on the 3rd go = 9.8 (source claim, Gripped).
- IFSC classic: rank by tops, then zones, then attempts to top, then attempts to zone ("2T3z 9 12") (source claim): https://gripped.com/?p=48138 . Remaining ties go to the previous round, then equal rank (source claim): https://www.kletterretter.com/en/boulder-world-cup-scoring-system . BMC Dual Showdown 2025 puts fewest attempts first, then tops, then zones (source claim): https://www.thebmc.co.uk/en/bmc-dual-showdown-climbing-competition-2025
- Redpoint "best N": only the best 5 scores on a card count (Mountain Games youth, source claim): https://mountaingames.com/event/youth-climbing-competition/ . ABS style: flash 100%, 2nd go 90%, later 80% (source claim): https://rrcarchives.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=72895 . LSU adds a flat +50 "flash points" (source claim).
- Leagues: Gravity Vault scores against each climber's handicap (current grade), counts the top 5 per week, adds +5 for a new set climbed within a week, uses team averages, and drops each team's lowest week (verified). Brooklyn: a top earns full points, a zone half, attempts aren't counted, the lowest week is dropped, and ties go to the hardest tops (source claim). TopLogger league: each top is worth 1000 divided by the number of climbers who topped it, plus 20% for a flash (source claim).
- (inference) That's at least 7 scoring schemes and at least 3 tie-break orders. But one record per climber per problem (attempts to zone, attempts to top, or none) is enough to compute every one-day format above. Handicaps, team averages and dropped weeks aren't.

## E3 Friction
- BUCS Nationals (UK universities, Sheffield, Feb 2026: 493 competitors, 30 blocs, 3 rounds) went from paper to Griptonite. The release contrasts it with "frantic tallying on spreadsheets" and lost or illegible scorecards, and claims zero entry errors (verified that the release says so; it's a sponsor press release, so these are vendor claims): https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/bucs-nationals-goes-digital-and-it-was-glorious/
- The Irish league's standing request to check results for errors after every round (verified, above) implies entry errors are expected (inference).
- BMC Leading Ladder: publishing results waited on walls posting back their scorecards, and one set went missing (source claim): https://services.thebmc.co.uk/bmc-leading-ladder-results-updated . On a USAC/JIBS forum, a competitor says judges misread a 4 as a 14 on a card (source claim): https://rockclimbing.com/forum/Climbing_Disciplines_C6/Competition_Climbing_F47/JIBS(USAC)_Nationals_P1014131
- Organisers build their own tools. Scottish National Bouldering League: the coordinator designed the scoring spreadsheet and entered the scores (source claim): https://www.ukclimbing.com/news/2016/03/scottish_national_bouldering_league_2015-2016_report-70327 . On a UKC thread, a climber wrote "a little program" for session scores, and Rochdale's monthly series is "hand your sheet in" (source claim): https://www.ukclimbing.com/forums/walls+training/competition_events-99225 . An Indonesian study found comp scoring done by hand in Excel (source claim): https://journal.unnes.ac.id/sju/jpes/article/view/48885
- (inference) A spreadsheet can rank IFSC classic with a packed helper key (tops x 10^10 + zones x 10^8 + (999 - top attempts) x 10^4 + (999 - zone attempts), then RANK.EQ). That's beyond most volunteers, and every format needs a different formula.
- Gap: no direct organiser complaint ("late night", "formula broke") was found. UGA treats Excel tallying as a routine volunteer role.

## E4 Alternatives (searched by the job's words)
- Free iPhone apps. Boulder Scoring (Graf x Apps): free, "No subscriptions, no per-event fees"; iPhone only (iOS 16.6+); climbers score themselves on their phones with a shared password, or judges score in judge mode; points with tops, zones and attempt penalties, redpoint values, tops-only, flash bonus, teams; a live leaderboard viewable on any device; export of the final standings; authentication mentioned; too few ratings to show (verified): https://apps.apple.com/ec/app/boulder-scoring/id6720720388 . SteepScores (Eaton Labs): free, iPhone only; climbers enter their own scores; leagues with handicaps, finals, judge menu and backup judge mode; 3 ratings (verified): https://apps.apple.com/app/id1568931412 . Sendkeeper (an iPhone logbook with a comp mode) and ClimbAlong (App Store) (source claim). No Android equivalents seen.
- Paid gym platforms, accounts required: TopLogger (the climber app is free; gyms run comps in it, gym-side price not found), Vertical-Life (Smart Climbing Gym, and a Result Service where judges use tablets), Griptonite (BUCS), BETA (Enterprise tier or a fee), theCrag Contests, MyClimb (all source claim). CRIMP: a judges' app plus a live results site, "works with bad connectivity", needs an organiser account, no price on its home page (verified): https://crimp.rocks/
- Generic: Leaderboarded and KeepTheScore publish "bouldering leaderboard" guides (free tier, spreadsheet upload or manual totals). They show totals and don't apply climbing rules (source claim): https://leaderboarded.com/blog/posts/bouldering-climbing-wall-leaderboard/ . QR-code phone forms: Gravity Vault posts a weekly QR code for climbers to submit their 5 best climbs (verified); a "Winter Boulder League: Round 2" JotForm (source claim): https://form.jotform.com/261660902533858
- Spreadsheets: no free climbing-specific template found, only generic scoreboards (round columns, total, rank, a notes column for tie-breaks) (source claim): https://spreadsheetdaddy.com/templates/scoreboard
- Gym front-desk software (Rock Gym Pro, Capitan): one search found no comp-scoring module (that doesn't prove there isn't one).

## E5 Strongest incumbent
- For the job as framed (paper cards keyed in at one desk): Excel or Google Sheets, which is what UGA's handbook prescribes (verified). It's free and can follow any rules, but the organiser builds every format, tie-break, best-N and flash bonus by hand, and no template exists.
- Strongest purpose-built tool: Boulder Scoring, free with no fees, covering most formats, with judge mode and export (verified). Limits: iPhone only, built around online live scoring with a shared password, and accounts exist (verified). Whether it needs a connection is my inference.
- Gyms already paying for TopLogger, Vertical-Life or Griptonite get comps built in, with live results on climbers' phones (source claim).

## E8 Strongest reason not to build
- The trend is phone self-scoring with live leaderboards. It's free (Boulder Scoring, SteepScores), built into gym platforms, and league nights have moved to QR forms (Gravity Vault, verified). A one-device static page can't put live standings on climbers' phones; at most it can show a second window on a screen at the desk (inference). The paper-desk niche may shrink.
- Format sprawl: 7 or more schemes and differing tie-break orders. Handicap and team leagues won't fit presets. A comp whose rules aren't covered gets nothing, while Excel can do anything (inference).
- The friction evidence is indirect: the sharpest pain quotes come from a vendor release, and no organiser complaint thread was found.
- (inference) Weighing these: real, but not fatal for one-day paper comps. The free apps are iPhone-only and online, and paper plus one desk is verified in 2024 documents.

## Single-device paper workflow: still common?
- Verified: UGA (paper cards, one Excel scorer, July 2024 handbook).
- Source claims: LSU 2025 and U Toledo printed cards; BMC youth series scorecards with a judge per bloc; Climb Scotland "CSFBC Scorecard 2025": https://www.climbscotland.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CSFBC-Scorecard-2025.pdf ; BMC Leading Ladder cards posted back by walls; Rochdale "hand your sheet in".
- Moving away: leagues (QR forms at Gravity Vault, verified; TopLogger in NZ and a JotForm, source claim) and big judged events (BUCS on Griptonite).
- (inference) Yes, it's common enough to matter at the small end Labs serves (one-day university, charity, youth-round and small-gym comps). It's weaker for multi-week leagues.

## Verdict
BUILD-worthy (moderate confidence), scoped to one-day comps scored from paper cards at one desk. Deciding reasons:
1. A verified, recurring job with a hard deadline: UGA has 6 categories of 20 to 25 problems, tallied in Excel, with awards 30 minutes after the last climb.
2. No free climbing-specific spreadsheet template or browser tool was found. The free purpose-built tools are iPhone-only, online, self-scoring apps; the rest are paid gym platforms with accounts.
3. It fits Labs cleanly: static, one device, works offline at a wall with poor signal, no accounts, data stays in the browser.
Wedge (inference): keyboard-first card entry; format presets (IFSC classic, IFSC 2025 points, attempt-tiered points like 10/7/4/2 + bonus, points plus flash %, best N) with the tie-break spelled out and shared places shown; categories; printable podium lists per category; CSV export.
Out of scope: handicap or team leagues, dropped weeks, live leaderboards on phones, entry on several devices.
Re-check in the main session: the tie-break rule for the IFSC 2025 points format (unverified); USA Climbing's redpoint rule text; whether Boulder Scoring's judge mode lets one organiser key in cards (that would make it the incumbent for iPhone users).
